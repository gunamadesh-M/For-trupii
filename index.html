To Trupii

I don't remember the exact moment you went from someone I know to someone I couldn't imagine my life without. It happened quietly, the way the most beautiful things always do.

You are so sweet, Trupii. Not the loud kind of sweet, but the kind that lives in tiny things: the way you care, the way you listen, the way you make people feel like they matter. Being around you feels like soft light through a window.

Thank you for your friendship. I am forever grateful for it. It has been a safe place, a smile on my hardest days, and a gift I never take for granted.

And here is my favorite part: I love you with conversations and without them. On the days we talk for hours and on the quiet days when we don't talk at all, you are still my person, and nothing about that changes. With you, even silence feels like home.

May this year wrap you in joy, bring you every dream you've been quietly holding, and love you back the way you love everyone else.

Happy Birthday, my sweetest Trupii.
